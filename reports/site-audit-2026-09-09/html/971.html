Why Choose ER of White Rock for Diagnostic Testing?

Laboratory results help your care team assess what may be causing your symptoms. A result is one part of an evaluation, alongside your health history and examination.

  • Understand the test: Ask which tests are being ordered and what questions they may help answer.
  • Discuss the results: A result outside the reference range does not always mean you have a medical condition. A result within the range does not rule out every problem.
  • Ask about next steps: Find out whether more testing or follow-up may be needed.

Comprehensive ER Laboratory Testing

Our state-of-the-art lab is equipped to run a massive array of critical diagnostic tests to pinpoint exactly what is happening inside your body.

When you are experiencing a medical emergency, rapid blood analysis is our first line of defense. Our on-site laboratory is equipped to run comprehensive emergency blood tests to instantly identify severe infections, organ failure, and metabolic crises.

  • Complete Blood Count (CBC): We instantly check your red and white blood cells to detect severe bacterial infections, acute blood loss, or dangerous immune responses.
  • Comprehensive Metabolic Panels: We evaluate your kidney function, liver health, and electrolyte balance to diagnose severe dehydration or diabetic emergencies.
  • Cardiac & Coagulation Panels: For patients with chest pain, we run immediate cardiac enzyme tests to detect active heart attacks and blood tests to rule out life-threatening blood clots.
  • Rapid Urinalysis: We rapidly analyze urine samples to detect painful urinary tract infections (UTIs), kidney stones, and severe kidney distress so we can administer IV antibiotics immediately.

If you suspect exposure to an infectious disease or are experiencing severe, painful symptoms, waiting days for a standard clinic appointment is incredibly stressful. We provide highly accurate, rapid, and completely confidential testing in a safe, private environment.

  • Comprehensive STD Panels: Our laboratory provides highly discreet, fast-acting diagnostic tests for Syphilis, Gonorrhea, Chlamydia, and HIV.
  • Rapid Viral Screening: We offer immediate testing for severe infectious diseases, including Hepatitis B and C, so you can understand your status fast.
  • Immediate Medical Intervention: Unlike a standalone commercial lab, our experienced emergency physicians are on-site to instantly review your results and prescribe the necessary antibiotics or antiviral medications before you leave our facility.
  • Total Patient Privacy: Your medical data and test results are strictly confidential, fully HIPAA-compliant, and processed securely right here in our White Rock facility.

RSV & Respiratory Panels

Cardiac Enzyme Testing

Cardiac Enzyme Testing

Unexplained chest pain is always a medical emergency. We measure specific protein levels in your blood to assess heart muscle damage and definitively diagnose a cardiac event.

Basic Metabolic Panel (BMP)

Basic Metabolic Panel (BMP)

A BMP provides a critical snapshot of your body's internal chemistry, including kidney function, blood sugar levels, and electrolyte balance. It is vital for diagnosing severe dehydration and diabetic crises.

Complete Blood Count (CBC)

Complete Blood Count (CBC)

A CBC is the foundation of emergency diagnostics. It allows our doctors to instantly detect systemic infections, severe anemia, and dangerous immune system disorders.

Liver Function Panel

Liver Function Panel

This panel checks vital liver enzymes and proteins to assess liver toxicity and detect severe conditions such as acute hepatitis, cirrhosis, and sudden organ damage.

Strep and Mono Testing

Strep and Mono Testing

Advanced Urinalysis

Advanced Urinalysis

A rapid urinalysis helps us diagnose painful urinary tract infections (UTIs), kidney stones, and complex metabolic disorders so we can administer the correct IV antibiotics.
