Richardson Emergency Care Questions

Do you serve Richardson?

Yes. Richardson families reach ER of White Rock by driving south on Plano Road or Jupiter Road through Lake Highlands. We are at 10705 Northwest Hwy, Dallas, TX 75238, open 24/7 with walk-in care. Call (469) 943-2939 on the way if you like.

What if the situation is life-threatening?

Call 911 first. For collapse, major bleeding, or stroke signs, paramedics start care immediately and choose the closest appropriate facility. Do not spend extra minutes driving. For serious but stable problems, come straight in at any hour.

When does a sore throat or cough need an ER?

Come in when swallowing or breathing becomes difficult, fever stays high, or symptoms worsen fast despite home care. The American College of Emergency Physicians offers Know When to Go guidance for judging severity. Mild symptoms usually fit urgent care or your doctor.

Will my insurance work here?

We accept most major private insurance plans. By federal law, every patient gets a medical screening exam regardless of ability to pay. As a freestanding emergency room, we do not participate in Medicare or Medicaid, and our team reviews costs with you before discharge.

Do I need a referral or appointment?

No. Emergency care is walk-in only, day and night. Come directly to 10705 Northwest Hwy whenever a medical problem cannot wait, weekends and holidays included. The full emergency team, imaging and lab run around the clock.
