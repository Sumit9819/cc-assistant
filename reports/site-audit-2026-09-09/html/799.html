When to Seek Gastrointestinal Disease Treatment

If you experience any of the following symptoms, visit ER of White Rock for immediate evaluation:

  • Persistent stomach pain or cramping
  • Unexplained weight loss
  • Difficulty swallowing
  • Chronic acid reflux or heartburn
  • Blood in stool or rectal bleeding
  • Severe nausea or vomiting
  • Changes in bowel habits

Ignoring these symptoms can lead to severe health complications. Early detection and treatment can help prevent serious conditions.
