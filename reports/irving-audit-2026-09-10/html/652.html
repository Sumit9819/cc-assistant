Cookie Policy

This Cookie Policy explains how Irving Health and Wellness Clinic uses cookies and similar technologies when you visit our website.

Last updated: December 22, 2025

COOKIE CONSENT

By continuing to use our website, you consent to our use of cookies in accordance with this Cookie Policy. You can manage your cookie preferences at any time through your browser settings.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your device (computer, smartphone, or tablet) when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently and provide information to website owners.

Cookies help us understand how you use our website, remember your preferences, and improve your overall experience. They can be "session cookies" (deleted when you close your browser) or "persistent cookies" (remain on your device until deleted or expired).

2. Types of Cookies We Use

Irving Health and Wellness Clinic uses several types of cookies to enhance your experience on our website:

Cookie TypePurposeDuration
Strictly Necessary CookiesEssential for website functionality, security, and navigation. These cannot be disabled.Session
Performance CookiesHelp us understand how visitors interact with our site by collecting anonymous data.1-2 years
Functional CookiesRemember your preferences and choices to provide enhanced features.1 year
Targeting/Advertising CookiesUsed to deliver relevant advertisements and track campaign effectiveness.1-2 years

Strictly Necessary Cookies

These cookies are essential for our website to function properly. They enable core functionality such as:

  • Secure login and authentication
  • Appointment booking system functionality
  • Shopping cart and checkout processes
  • Security and fraud prevention
  • Load balancing to ensure website stability

Without these cookies, services you have requested cannot be provided. These cookies do not gather information about you for marketing purposes.

Performance and Analytics Cookies

These cookies collect information about how visitors use our website, including:

  • Which pages are visited most often
  • Time spent on different pages
  • Error messages received
  • How users navigate through the site

This information is aggregated and anonymous. We use services like Google Analytics to help us improve website performance and user experience.

Functional Cookies

These cookies allow our website to remember choices you make and provide enhanced, personalized features:

  • Remembering your login details (if you choose)
  • Storing your preferences for appointment types
  • Remembering language or region preferences
  • Customizing content based on your previous visits

Targeting and Advertising Cookies

These cookies are used to deliver advertisements that are relevant to you and your interests. They may be set by our advertising partners and are used to:

  • Build a profile of your interests
  • Show relevant ads on other websites you visit
  • Measure the effectiveness of advertising campaigns
  • Limit the number of times you see an advertisement

These cookies do not store directly personal information but are based on uniquely identifying your browser and device.

3. Third-Party Cookies

In addition to our own cookies, we use various third-party services that may set cookies on your device. These include:

Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors interact with our website. Google Analytics uses cookies to collect anonymous information including the number of visitors, where visitors have come from, and the pages they visited. This helps us improve our website and services.

For more information about Google Analytics cookies, visit Google's Privacy Policy.

Social Media Plugins

Our website includes social media features (such as Facebook, Instagram, and Twitter buttons) that may set cookies to enable you to share content. These features may collect your IP address and which page you're visiting, and may set a cookie to enable the feature to function properly.

Advertising Networks

We may work with third-party advertising networks to display ads on our website or to manage our advertising on other sites. These partners may use cookies to collect information about your activities to provide you with targeted advertising.

4. Managing Your Cookie Preferences

You have the right to decide whether to accept or reject cookies. You can exercise your cookie preferences in several ways:

Browser Settings

Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings. You can set your browser to:

  • Block all cookies
  • Block third-party cookies only
  • Delete cookies when you close your browser
  • Alert you when a website tries to set a cookie

Instructions for managing cookies in popular browsers:

  • Google Chrome: Settings, then Privacy and Security, then Cookies and other site data
  • Safari: Preferences, then Privacy, then Manage Website Data
  • Firefox: Options, then Privacy & Security, then Cookies and Site Data
  • Microsoft Edge: Settings, then Privacy, Search, and Services, then Cookies

Opt-Out Tools

You can opt out of interest-based advertising by visiting:

  • Digital Advertising Alliance (DAA): www.aboutads.info/choices
  • Network Advertising Initiative (NAI): www.networkadvertising.org/choices
  • Google Ads Settings: www.google.com/settings/ads

Impact of Disabling Cookies

Please note that if you disable or refuse cookies, some parts of our website may become inaccessible or not function properly. Specifically, you may not be able to:

  • Book appointments online
  • Save your preferences
  • Access certain personalized features
  • Use shopping cart functionality

5. Mobile Devices

If you access our website through a mobile device, you can manage cookies through your device settings:

  • iOS: Settings, then Safari, then Block All Cookies
  • Android: Settings, then Privacy, then Clear browsing data

Mobile apps may also use mobile identifiers for advertising and analytics. You can manage these through your device's privacy settings or opt out of personalized advertising.

6. Do Not Track Signals

Some browsers include a "Do Not Track" (DNT) feature that signals to websites that you do not want to have your online activity tracked. Currently, there is no industry standard for how to respond to DNT signals.

Our website does not currently respond to DNT signals. However, you can use the cookie management tools described above to control tracking on our site.

7. Children's Privacy

Our website is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through cookies or any other means. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with information, please contact us immediately.

8. Updates to This Cookie Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time to reflect changes in technology, legislation, our business operations, or for other operational, legal, or regulatory reasons.

When we make changes, we will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy. We encourage you to review this Cookie Policy periodically to stay informed about how we use cookies.

If we make material changes to this policy, we may notify you by posting a notice on our website homepage or by sending you an email (if you have provided your email address).

9. Your Rights Under Privacy Laws

Depending on your location, you may have certain rights regarding cookies and personal data:

  • Right to Access: Request information about what data has been collected through cookies
  • Right to Deletion: Request deletion of data collected through cookies
  • Right to Opt-Out: Opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information
  • Right to Non-Discrimination: Exercise your rights without discrimination

For more information about your privacy rights, please see our Privacy Policy or contact us using the information below.
