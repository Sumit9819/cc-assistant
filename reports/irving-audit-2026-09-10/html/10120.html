What Are Dermal Fillers?

Dermal fillers are smooth hyaluronic acid gels injected under the skin to restore volume your face loses with age. The FDA regulates fillers as medical devices, and we use FDA-approved hyaluronic acid products only.

Results are visible immediately and settle fully within about two weeks. Because hyaluronic acid occurs naturally in your skin, these fillers integrate softly and can be dissolved later if your goals change.

Fillers are one part of our aesthetic treatments in Irving, and they pair well with Botox in the same visit.

Dermal Filler Aftercare

The first 48 hours decide how evenly your filler settles. Keep it simple:

DO

  • Apply a cool compress in short intervals to calm swelling
  • Sleep slightly elevated on your back the first night
  • Keep skincare gentle for the first 24 hours
  • Come to your follow-up so we can check how the filler settled

DON'T

  • No strenuous exercise or alcohol for 24 hours
  • No makeup directly on injection sites for 24 hours
  • No facials, massages, or firm pressure on the area for one week
  • No saunas or intense heat for 48 hours

Dermal Filler Questions, Answered

Do dermal filler injections hurt?

Most patients describe filler injections as brief pressure rather than sharp pain. We apply a topical numbing cream first, and most FDA-approved hyaluronic acid fillers also contain lidocaine, a local anesthetic that numbs as we work. Sensitive areas like the lips may feel more, so we adjust pacing and numbing to keep you comfortable.

How long do dermal fillers last?

Most hyaluronic acid fillers last 9 to 18 months, depending on the area treated and the product used. High-movement areas like the lips tend to break down filler faster than the cheeks or jawline. At your follow-up we track how your result holds so you can plan touch-ups on your own schedule.

What is the downtime after fillers?

Plan for minor swelling or redness at the injection sites for 24 to 48 hours. Most patients return to work the same day. We ask you to avoid strenuous exercise, alcohol, and firm pressure on the treated area for the first day so the filler settles evenly.

Can dermal fillers be reversed?

Yes. Hyaluronic acid fillers can be dissolved with hyaluronidase, an enzyme injected into the treated area if you want to adjust or remove your result. This reversibility is a key safety advantage of hyaluronic acid fillers, and one reason we use them as our standard at the Irving clinic.

Can I get Botox and fillers in the same visit?

Yes. Botox relaxes the muscles that create expression lines, while fillers restore volume in a different layer of tissue. Because the two treatments work in different ways, they can be done safely in the same appointment. Many patients combine them for a complete refresh in one visit.
