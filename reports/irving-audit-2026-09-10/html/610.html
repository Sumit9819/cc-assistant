A Personalized Approach to Your Metabolism

Standard diets often apply a one size fits all approach that ignores your individual metabolic needs. We focus on identifying and addressing the root causes of weight retention, including insulin resistance patterns often seen with PCOS, so you can achieve lasting vitality. Our programs are designed to assist you with:

Stubborn Weight

Many patients arrive after years of diet plateaus that nutrition alone cannot break. We use advanced GLP-1 therapies to address the underlying metabolic resistance, including insulin sensitivity, hormonal imbalances, and your body's tendency to defend a higher set-point weight.

Metabolic Optimization

A sluggish metabolism is often the result of years of restrictive dieting, hormonal shifts, or undiagnosed thyroid issues. We use custom nutrient protocols, full thyroid testing, and GLP-1 medication to reignite metabolic rate so your body burns calories efficiently again.

Appetite Regulation

Constant food noise and unmanageable cravings drive most failed weight loss attempts. GLP-1 medications quiet appetite signals in your brain so you can make food choices comfortably, without the willpower battle that traditional diets demand.

Energy Restoration

Low energy on a calorie deficit is the leading reason patients quit weight loss programs. Our protocol pairs GLP-1 with proper nutrition, hydration, and supplementation so you maintain daily stamina and stay engaged in your lifestyle.

Body Composition

Losing weight on the scale is not the same as losing fat. Our supervised protocol uses 3D body scans, lean-muscle tracking, and personalized nutrition to ensure you lose fat while preserving the muscle that protects your long-term metabolism.

How Does GLP-1 Therapy Work?

Many traditional weight loss programs push severe calorie deficits that can actually harm your metabolism and lead to muscle loss. Our weight loss clinic in Irving takes a metabolic first approach.

We utilize advanced GLP-1 receptor agonists, specifically compounded semaglutide (the active ingredient in Wegovy and Ozempic) and compounded tirzepatide (the active ingredient in Mounjaro and Zepbound). These medications work with your body to regulate appetite and improve insulin sensitivity.

Patients sometimes call these the “skinny shot,” but they are FDA-cleared metabolic medications backed by clinical research. The landmark STEP-1 trial published in The New England Journal of Medicine reported an average 14.9% body weight reduction over 68 weeks in patients treated with semaglutide.

This scientific approach helps shift your body away from fat storage mode while quieting the constant mental cravings for food, allowing you to make healthier choices comfortably.

What's the difference between Semaglutide and Tirzepatide?

Semaglutide (the active ingredient in Wegovy and Ozempic) is a single-pathway GLP-1 receptor agonist that helps regulate appetite and insulin sensitivity. Tirzepatide (the active ingredient in Mounjaro and Zepbound) is a dual GLP-1 plus GIP receptor agonist, which acts on two metabolic pathways simultaneously. In clinical trials, Tirzepatide produces faster and greater weight loss for most patients, while Semaglutide is the most-studied of the GLP-1 medications. Your Board Certified APRN matches the medication to your metabolic profile, tolerance, and goals.

How much weight will I lose per month?

Results vary by individual, but a realistic average is 3 to 6 pounds per month during the first three months, with the largest losses typically occurring after dose titration. The landmark STEP-1 trial reported an average 14.9% body weight reduction over 68 weeks on semaglutide; SURMOUNT-1 reported 20-22% on tirzepatide. Your starting weight, biology, and adherence to nutritional guidance will affect your individual outcome.

What if I have side effects like nausea?

Mild to moderate nausea is the most common side effect, especially during the first two to four weeks after starting or increasing your dose. Our APRN uses a conservative titration schedule to minimize discomfort, and we provide a side-effect management protocol that includes hydration, small frequent meals, and timing your injection relative to your typical eating pattern. Side effects that do not resolve are reviewed and the dose can be adjusted.

Am I a candidate for GLP-1 therapy?

GLP-1 medications are FDA-approved for adults with a BMI of 30 or higher, or 27 or higher with a weight-related condition such as type 2 diabetes, prediabetes, or hypertension. They are not appropriate for patients who are pregnant or breastfeeding, who have a personal or family history of medullary thyroid carcinoma, or who have a history of pancreatitis. Your Board Certified APRN reviews your medical history before any prescription is written.

What happens when I stop taking GLP-1?

Some weight regain after stopping is common because the medication is regulating your appetite and insulin signaling. The goal of our program is not lifelong medication, it is metabolic re-training. Our nutritional and lifestyle support is designed to help you maintain results after the medication phase ends. Some patients choose to continue at a lower maintenance dose, and our APRN discusses long-term planning with you well before your program ends.
