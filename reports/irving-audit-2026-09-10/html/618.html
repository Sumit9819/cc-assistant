Understanding Facial Aging and Volume Loss

Facial aging goes far beyond simple surface wrinkles. It is fundamentally an issue of volume loss and bone resorption, and our anti-aging program in Irving is built around that science.

As we age, our facial skeletal structure actually changes, and the fat pads beneath our skin begin to shift downward. Research published in the Journal of the American Academy of Dermatology confirms that midface volume loss starts in the late twenties and accelerates after age 40.

Topical skincare only sits on the surface of your skin and absolutely cannot replace lost bone density or structural fat.

This is why we utilize structural fillers (including hyaluronic-acid dermal fillers and biostimulators like Skinvive) to rebuild your facial foundation, and precision neurotoxins to stop the repetitive movements that etch permanent lines into your skin.

Frequently Asked Questions About Aesthetic Treatments

Common questions about Irving Wellness’ services:

Do I need an appointment for aesthetic treatments?

Yes, an appointment is required. Aesthetic treatments require precise anatomy mapping and a thorough consultation to ensure your safety and optimal results. This keeps your visit focused and cuts down on waiting.

What should I bring to my aesthetic consultation?

Please bring a photo ID and a list of any current medications. We also highly recommend bringing a short note with your top two or three aesthetic goals, as it is very easy to forget exactly what you meant to say once you are in the treatment room. If possible, arriving with a clean, makeup free face is helpful for your skin assessment.

How long does the first appointment take?

Plan for 30 to 60 minutes, depending on what you need. If labs or a treatment are part of the visit, it can run longer.

Do you take walk-ins?

Sometimes, but it depends on the day and the service. If you show up and we’re packed, we’ll still try to help you schedule something soon.

What is the typical recovery time after injectables and laser treatments?

Botox and dermal fillers cause minor swelling for 24 to 48 hours but most patients return to work the same day. Laser hair removal causes redness for 4 to 6 hours and rare blistering on darker skin tones. Laser Genesis sessions need no downtime at all, so you can schedule your first treatment on a workday.

Skin resurfacing recovery depends on depth. Light resurfacing is 3 days of pinkness, medium-depth is 5 to 7 days of peeling, and deeper protocols can take up to 14 days. We schedule deeper resurfacing around your social calendar.

Is microneedling with PRP worth the cost premium over plain microneedling?

Plain microneedling costs $425 per treatment and works well for general texture, fine lines, and pore refinement. Adding PRP roughly doubles the price to $825 and extends recovery by 1 to 2 days because the platelet serum needs time to absorb.

PRP is most worth it for acne scars and deeper texture concerns where the growth factors accelerate collagen rebuilding. For maintenance or first-time microneedling, plain treatments deliver solid results at half the cost.

Can I combine Botox, fillers, and microneedling in one visit?

Botox and dermal fillers can be done in the same appointment because they target different layers and do not interfere with each other. Microneedling should be scheduled at least 2 weeks before or 2 weeks after injectables, since the needling can disturb how the filler settles.

We map your full sequence at consultation so the timeline fits your schedule and your skin’s response window.

How long do results last with Botox vs dermal fillers vs Skinvive?

Botox lasts 3 to 4 months because it relaxes the muscles that cause expression wrinkles, and those muscles gradually reactivate. Hyaluronic-acid dermal fillers last 9 to 18 months depending on the area and the specific product.

Skinvive is a skin-quality booster rather than a structural filler, with results lasting about 6 months. Most patients combine all three on a staggered schedule for steady-state results.
