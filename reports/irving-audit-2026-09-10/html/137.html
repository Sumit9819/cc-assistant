Why Choose IV Therapy Over Oral Supplements?

The difference between a daily vitamin and an IV drip is not just price; it is biology.

When you take oral supplements with water, the nutrients must pass through your digestive system where stomach acids break them down. This typically results in an absorption rate of only 20 percent. IV therapy provides a massive clinical advantage by delivering pharmaceutical-grade nutrients directly into your bloodstream. This ensures 100% absorption and immediate cellular uptake, so you feel the benefits instantly.

Oral Supplements (Pills/Water)

Absorption 20%

Must pass through the digestive system, where acids break down nutrients.

IV Therapy (Irving Health & Wellness)

Absorption 100%

Delivered directly into the bloodstream for immediate cellular uptake.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about Irving Wellness’ services:

Do I need an appointment for an IV drip?

Most of the time, yes. Scheduling keeps your visit focused and reduces wait times. However, we know hangovers and migraines happen unexpectedly. If you need a same-day visit, please call us first so we can quickly tell you our availability.

What should I bring to my first visit?

You should bring:

  • A photo ID
  • Any current medication list
  • Recent labs, if you have them
  • A short note with your top 2 or 3 goals

That last one helps more than people expect. When you’re in the room, it’s easy to forget what you meant to say.

How long does an IV infusion take?

Please plan for 45 to 60 minutes. This gives us ample time to check your vitals, comfortably place the IV, and allow the fluids to safely enter your bloodstream while you relax.

Does the IV insertion hurt?

We strive to make the process as painless as possible. Our vascular experts use specialized pediatric gauge needles, which are much smaller than standard hospital needles, ensuring you barely feel a pinch.
