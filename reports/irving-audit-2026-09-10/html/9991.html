What Makes Microneedling FDA-Cleared?

Microneedling devices used in our clinic are FDA-cleared as Class II medical devices for treating acne scars, fine lines, stretch marks, and pigmentation concerns under regulatory oversight on needle depth, sterility, and infection control. Three numbers worth knowing.

FDA Class II

Medical-device classification when needle depth exceeds 0.3mm. Subject to FDA premarket clearance and post-market device-tracking requirements.

0.5 to 2.5 mm

Approved needle depths used for facial and body indications. We adjust depth based on the area being treated, skin thickness, and your tolerance.

3 to 6 sessions

Standard series for full collagen remodeling. Sessions are spaced 4 to 6 weeks apart so new collagen has time to form before the next pass.

What Are the Most Common Microneedling Myths?

Here are the four misconceptions we hear most often, with the clinical context behind each.

Myth: It's the same as a derma roller

Fact: Professional microneedling uses sterile single-use cartridges with needles up to 2.5 mm under controlled depth. At-home derma rollers rarely reach the dermis and can cross-contaminate.

Myth: More bleeding equals better results

Fact: Pinpoint bleeding signals correct dermal depth, not efficacy. Collagen induction depends on controlled microinjury, not blood volume.

Myth: Sensitive skin cannot be treated

Fact: Sensitive skin can be treated with shorter needle depths and longer healing intervals. We screen for active eczema, rosacea flares, and isotretinoin use within the past 6 months.

Myth: Results show up overnight

Fact: New collagen takes 4 to 6 weeks to remodel. Peak results from a single session appear at month 3 to 6. A series of 3 to 6 sessions produces the strongest long-term outcome.

PATIENT EXPERIENCE

Your Microneedling Appointment: What to Expect

We have designed the microneedling appointment to feel calm, predictable, and unhurried. Here is a step-by-step guide to your visit so you know exactly what to anticipate.

Skin Consultation

Your nurse reviews your skin history, current routine, sun exposure, and any active conditions. We confirm microneedling is the right fit for your skin and explain the plan before we begin.

The Treatment

Cleanse, topical numbing applied, then the microneedling device passes across the treatment area in controlled rows. Most patients describe a warm, prickly sensation that fades quickly.

Aftercare

Mild redness or warmth may follow for 24 to 48 hours. Apply broad-spectrum SPF, skip retinoids and exfoliating acids for 48 hours, and resume your normal routine the same day or next morning.

How Does the Microneedling Healing Process Work?

Here is the typical recovery and remodeling timeline for a standard 1.0 to 1.5 mm microneedling session.

Hours 0 to 24

Pinpoint bleeding stops within hours. Skin appears flushed with mild swelling, similar to a moderate sunburn.

Days 1 to 3

Redness fades to a mild pink. Skin feels tight, slightly rough, and warm. Use gentle moisturizer and SPF only.

Days 4 to 7

Light sloughing of dead skin reveals smoother texture. Resume normal skincare; avoid retinoids and chemical acids.

Weeks 2 to 4

Initial brightness and refined pores become visible. Collagen remodeling begins. Avoid direct sun exposure to protect new skin.

Months 3 to 6

Peak collagen and elastin remodeling produces lifted, smooth skin. Schedule maintenance every 4 to 6 months or after a 3-session series.

What is microneedling and how does it work?

Microneedling, also called collagen induction therapy, uses a medical-grade device with very fine sterile needles to create thousands of controlled micro-channels in the upper layer of the skin. Your body responds by producing new collagen and elastin during the healing process, which gradually smooths texture, softens scars, and improves overall tone. The actual treatment time is roughly 30 to 45 minutes after numbing.

How many microneedling sessions will I need?

Most patients see meaningful improvement after a series of 3 sessions spaced 4 to 6 weeks apart, which is why we offer both single treatments and 3-session packages. Patients treating deeper acne scarring or significant texture concerns may benefit from 4 to 6 sessions, and we will outline a realistic plan during your consultation based on your skin and goals.

What is the downtime after microneedling?

Downtime is minimal for most patients. Expect mild redness and a sunburn-like warmth for 24 to 48 hours, with possible light flaking around day 3. Most people return to work or normal activities the next day. Avoid retinoids, exfoliating acids, and direct sun exposure for at least 48 hours, and apply broad-spectrum SPF 30 or higher whenever you go outside.

Should I book an appointment for microneedling?

Yes. Microneedling is a planned procedure, so booking in advance protects your spot. It also gives us time to review your skin history before the session. If you have a special event coming up, call us at least 2 to 3 weeks ahead. That way we can sequence your treatments for the best result on the day that matters.

Is microneedling safe for sensitive skin?

For most patients with sensitive skin, yes. Microneedling is well tolerated when performed by a trained clinician at appropriate needle depths. During your consultation we review any history of rosacea, active acne flares, eczema, recent isotretinoin use, or current cold sores. Those situations change either the timing or the depth we choose. If microneedling is not the right fit on the day of your visit, we will tell you upfront and discuss alternatives.
