Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about Irving Health and Wellness’ services:

Do I need an appointment at your Irving health center?

Most of the time, yes. It keeps your visit focused, and it cuts down on waiting. If you want same-day, call first. We can usually tell you quickly what’s realistic.

What should I bring to my first visit?

You should bring:

  • A photo ID
  • Any current medication list
  • Recent labs, if you have them
  • A short note with your top 2 or 3 goals

That last one helps more than people expect. When you’re in the room, it’s easy to forget what you meant to say.

How long does the first appointment take?

Plan for 30 to 60 minutes, depending on what you need. If labs or a treatment are part of the visit, it can run longer.

Do you take walk-ins?

Yes! We welcome walk-in clients. While appointments are recommended to guarantee a specific time, feel free to stop by, and our team will do our best to accommodate you immediately or find the earliest available opening that day.
