What Do New Patients Ask Most Often?

Straight answers from our clinical team, based on what patients ask us every week.

How long is the first appointment?

Plan for 45 minutes for your initial consultation. We review your history, current concerns, and goals before discussing options. Most patients leave with a clear protocol and a timeline for results. Same-week openings are usually available.

Do you accept insurance?

Most wellness services are paid out of pocket. We provide itemized superbills for HSA and FSA submission, which many of our patients use. Our team can explain what your plan typically covers before any treatment, so there are no surprises.

Do I need a referral?

No referral is needed for any service we offer. Walk-ins are welcome though appointments guarantee your time slot. The fastest path to a first visit is the booking link or a call to 972-891-8650.

Are your protocols evidence-based?

Yes. Every treatment plan follows clinical guidance from authorities including the Endocrine Society, the American Academy of Dermatology, and FDA-approved standards. Lori reviews each plan personally before treatment begins.

How is your clinic different from a typical med spa?

Our clinic is medically supervised and led by a Board-Certified Family Nurse Practitioner with 17+ years of clinical experience. Standard med spas often skip the clinical workup. We start with labs and goals, then build the plan around your biology rather than a fixed menu.

What conditions do you address most often?

Chronic fatigue, perimenopausal hormone shifts, low testosterone, stubborn weight that resists diet and exercise, brain fog, sluggish thyroid function, and skin concerns like fine lines, hyperpigmentation, and acne scarring. Each protocol is tailored to your specific labs and history.

What should I bring to my first visit?

Bring a photo ID, any current medication list, recent lab results if you have them, and a short note with your top two or three goals. The goals note matters more than people expect; it keeps the conversation focused.

Will my information stay private?

Yes. Your health records, lab work, and visit notes are handled under HIPAA-aligned data practices. We never share patient information with third parties for marketing or any other reason. Records are accessible only to your care team.

Are you accepting new patients?

Yes. We welcome new patients across all service lines and accept same-week first appointments when capacity allows. Booking is available online at our scheduling page or by calling 972-891-8650 during business hours, Monday through Friday.

What Is Our Promise to You?

Our promise is to answer your questions honestly. Bring any question to your first consultation and you will get a straight answer from our clinical team.
