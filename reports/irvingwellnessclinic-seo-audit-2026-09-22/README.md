## The one-paragraph version

This site is not broken and does not need rescuing. Technically it is in the top decile:
clean canonicals, correct sitemaps, noindexed archives, working 301s, zero schema errors,
zero missing or duplicate meta across 82 URLs, CLS of zero, and a robots.txt that actively
invites AI crawlers. Content quality averages 91.5 with real clinician attribution and
.gov citations no local rival matches. What holds it back is not on the page. It is a
domain authority of 10 against a market leader at 31, and a click-conversion problem where
101,469 impressions produced 100 clicks. And the authority number is worse than it looks:
the backlink profile is roughly two real editorial links plus about a hundred PBN spam
links. Before writing another article, find out whether those 61,676 position-one
zero-click impressions are converting through Google Business Profile, because if they
are, this site performs far better than Search Console suggests, and the strategy should
shift from content production to citations and reviews.
