# Link and Authority Brief
**Site:** Irving Health and Wellness Clinic · **Date:** 2026-09-22

## Two blockers before building anything

**1. Resolve the suite number.** The site says Suite 100; the PRNewswire release says Suite 110, which is ER of Irving's suite in the same building. Every citation locks in whichever you use. Twenty listings on the wrong NAP is worse than none, because cleanup is manual.

**2. Answer the PBN question.** If nobody bought those links, ignore them. If someone did, end that vendor relationship before adding clean links on top.

## Canonical NAP block

Once the suite is confirmed, this exact string goes everywhere, byte-identical. No abbreviation drift, no "Ste" on one and "Suite" on another.

```
Irving Health and Wellness Clinic
8200 N MacArthur Blvd Suite 100
Irving, TX 75063
(972) 891-8650
https://irvingwellnessclinic.com
```

NAP consistency is the actual local ranking factor here, not link equity. Most of these listings are nofollow and that is fine. You are building entity confirmation, not PageRank.

## Anchor text

On Tier 1 you do NOT choose the anchor. Directories use the business name or a bare URL automatically. That is correct and natural. Anchor strategy only applies to editorial and partner links you can influence.

| Anchor type | Share | Exact strings |
|---|---|---|
| Branded | 55% | `Irving Health and Wellness Clinic` / `Irving Health & Wellness Clinic` |
| Naked URL | 20% | `irvingwellnessclinic.com` |
| Person | 10% | `Lori Secerovic, APRN, FNP-C` |
| Generic | 10% | `visit the clinic website` / `learn more` / `book a consultation` |
| Partial-match geo | 5% | `medical weight loss in Irving` / `IV therapy in Irving, TX` |

NEVER use `best IV therapy Irving`, `weight loss clinic near me`, or anything keyword-stuffed. The existing anchor profile is already 100% keyword-stuffed spam. Adding more commercial-anchor signal is the one thing that could turn a harmless spam pattern into a real problem. Branded anchors actively dilute it.

# Tier 2: Local TV (highest value)

Worth more than all of Tier 1 combined. Competitor happyhealthwellness.com earned kxan.com (DA 82) this way.

**Primary target:** WFAA *Good Morning Texas*, weekdays 9 to 10am, Dallas. Co-hosted by Erin Hunter, contributor Paige McCoy. https://www.wfaa.com/gmt

**Also:** NBC5 DFW, KTVT CBS11, FOX4 *Good Day*.

**How to pitch.** These shows book experts on news hooks, never businesses on promotion. "Our clinic offers IV therapy" gets deleted. A pitch tied to something in the news gets booked.

Working angles for Lori:
- "Compounded GLP-1s after the shortage ended: what DFW patients should ask before buying" (real regulatory news, real consumer risk, she is credentialed to answer)
- "What that IV drip actually does, and what it does not" (the evidence-based content already exists to back it)
- Texas summer heat and dehydration (seasonal, recurring, easy yes)

**Include:** one paragraph on the hook, two sentences on why Lori qualifies (APRN, FNP-C, MSN, 17 years, University of Chicago Medical Center), three bullet talking points, and an offer of clinic b-roll. Under 200 words.

**Expect a low hit rate.** Pitch four stations, follow up once, repeat monthly with a fresh hook. One booking a quarter is good, and each is a DA 80+ link plus reusable video.

# Reviews: the underused lever

Currently 50 Google reviews at 5 stars. Review velocity is the strongest local-pack input available and is fully within the clinic's control, unlike links.

Target 10 to 15 new reviews per month. Ask at the end of every visit using the link already in the site footer: `g.page/r/CaSU-i78QKBpEBM/review`. A clinic seeing 500+ patients should be well past 50 reviews.

# What not to do

- No paid link packages, guest-post networks, or "DA 50 backlinks" offers. There is a live example on this domain of what that produces.
- No exact-match commercial anchors.
- Do not chase DA as a metric. For a single-location clinic the map pack decides revenue, and citations plus reviews move it. DA may barely move while bookings do.

# Sequencing

- **Weeks 3 to 4:** Tier 1 general business. Chamber membership. Fix Focus Health cross-links.

Realistic 90-day target: 15 to 20 genuine referring domains against the 2 that exist now, plus 30+ new reviews.
