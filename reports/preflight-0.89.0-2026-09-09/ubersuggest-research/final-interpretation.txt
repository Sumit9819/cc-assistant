The single most important finding: **`/emergency-lab-testing-white-rock-tx/` is already the site's strongest content asset.** It ranks for ~18 lab-timing queries at positions 8–30, including "how long does bloodwork take" (3,600 US vol, pos 14), "how long does a blood test take in the er" (880, pos 11), and "how long does blood work take in the er" (880, pos 9).
