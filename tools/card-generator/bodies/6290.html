What to Expect During an IV Drip Session

The process step-by-step

An IV session is simple, safe, and comfortable. You’ll be seated in a relaxed setting while a licensed provider inserts a small catheter into your vein, usually in your arm or hand. Over 30–60 minutes, a blend of vitamins, minerals, and fluids flows directly into your bloodstream. Many patients read, relax, or listen to music during the session. You can typically return to your normal routine immediately after.

Safety and professional supervision

IV therapy should always be performed by trained medical professionals using sterile techniques. Mild side effects, such as temporary bruising or lightheadedness, are rare. Always discuss your health history and medications with your provider before treatment.
