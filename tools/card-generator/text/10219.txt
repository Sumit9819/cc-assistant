A double chin is stubborn in a way that frustrates people. It often stays put through weight loss, and it shows up in photos from angles nobody chooses.

This guide covers what actually causes submental fat, how the non-surgical options compare, and who Kybella suits.

## What causes a double chin?

Submental fat sits in a pocket beneath the jawline. Genetics decide how much you store there and how visible it is.

Weight gain adds to it, but weight is not the whole story. Plenty of lean people have one, and plenty of people lose weight everywhere except under the chin. Age contributes too, since skin loses elasticity and the area softens.

## What are the non-surgical options for submental fat?

There are three broad routes. An injectable that destroys the fat cells, a device that freezes them, and surgery that removes them.

Only one of those is a drug approved specifically for this area. Our clinic offers the injectable route, which is Kybella.

## How does Kybella break down fat under the chin?

Kybella is deoxycholic acid. Your body already makes it, and its normal job is helping you absorb dietary fat.

Injected into a fat pocket, it works as a cytolytic drug. It physically breaks down the fat cell membrane, and the destroyed cells are cleared by the body over the following weeks.

## Is Kybella FDA approved?

Yes. The FDA approved it in April 2015 for moderate to severe fat below the chin in adults.

Approval was based on two placebo-controlled Phase III trials involving more than 1,000 adults. It remains the only injectable approved for this specific use.

## How many Kybella treatments will you need?

More than one, in almost every case. The FDA label allows up to six treatments, spaced at least one month apart.

In the clinical trials, 59% of patients received all six. Your provider maps the area and decides the number based on how much fat is there and how you respond to the first round.

## What happens during a Kybella appointment?

The area is marked with a grid so the injections are evenly spaced. Numbing is applied first, and ice helps with comfort.

The label permits up to 50 small injections in a single session. The injecting itself takes around 15 to 20 minutes.

## Are Kybella results permanent?

The treated fat cells are destroyed and do not come back. In that sense the result is lasting.

You can still gain weight, and the remaining cells in the area can enlarge. Most people who keep their weight stable do not need repeat rounds.

## What are the side effects of Kybella?

Most reactions are local and temporary. The trial data gives useful numbers rather than vague reassurance.

- Bruising affected 72% of trial subjects

- Swelling, pain, numbness, and firmness at the site are common

- Nerve injury causing an uneven smile occurred in 4%, and every case resolved on its own

### How long did the rarer effects last?

In the trials, nerve-related weakness resolved with a median of 44 days. Difficulty swallowing occurred in 2% of subjects and resolved with a median of 3 days.

## Who is not a good candidate for Kybella?

It is not for everyone. The label specifically warns against use where infection is present at the injection site.

Anyone with a current or previous history of difficulty swallowing should avoid it, since treatment can worsen that. It is also not appropriate if your concern is loose skin rather than fat.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Does Kybella tighten loose skin?

No. It treats fat only. If skin laxity is the main issue, a fat treatment will not improve it and may make it more noticeable. This is the single most common reason we tell someone Kybella is not right for them.

### How soon will you see results?

Most people see a visible change after two to four treatments. Because swelling masks the result early on, judge progress about a month after each session rather than in the first week.

### Does Kybella hurt?

There is stinging and burning during the injections and for a short time afterward. Numbing cream and ice make it manageable. Most people describe it as uncomfortable rather than severe.

### Can Kybella be used elsewhere on the body?

Fat below the chin is the only FDA-approved use. Anything else is off-label, and we do not offer it that way.

### Can you combine Kybella with other treatments?

Yes, though usually not on the same day. Many people pair it with Botox or dermal fillers for the wider jawline and lower face.

## Is Kybella right for you?

It suits people close to their goal weight with a defined pocket of fat under the chin and reasonable skin quality. It does not suit people whose main concern is sagging.
