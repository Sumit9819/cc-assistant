Skinvive gets called "a filler" constantly, including by people selling it. It is not one, and the confusion leads people to book the wrong treatment.

Both are hyaluronic acid injectables. What they do with that hyaluronic acid is completely different, and that difference decides which one you need.

## What is Skinvive and how is it different from filler?

Skinvive by Juvederm is a hyaluronic acid microdroplet injectable. The FDA approved it in May 2023 to improve skin smoothness of the cheeks in adults over 21, and it was the first product of its kind approved in the US.

Rather than filling a specific line or hollow, it is placed as many tiny droplets across the treatment area. The goal is hydration, elasticity, and surface smoothness rather than shape.

## Does Skinvive add volume to the face?

No. This is the single most important thing to understand about it.

The droplets are too small and placed too superficially to lift or plump anything. If your concern is a deep fold, a flat cheek, or a thin lip, Skinvive is the wrong tool no matter how well it is marketed.

## What skin concerns does Skinvive treat?

It suits skin that looks tired rather than sunken. Dullness, fine crepey texture, and dehydration that skincare alone has not fixed.

In the clinical study behind the approval, patients reported higher satisfaction with how glowing, hydrated, refreshed, and healthy their skin looked. Those words describe surface quality, not shape, which is exactly the product's lane.
"One key way to improve skin quality is by enhancing hydration. SKINVIVE by JUVEDERM is truly innovative because it works beneath the skin's surface to increase skin hydration improving skin quality," said Macrene Alexiades, MD, PhD, dual US-EU board-certified dermatologist and lead investigator of the Skinvive clinical trial, in AbbVie's approval announcement.

## How is Skinvive injected?

It is placed as a series of very small injections spaced roughly half a centimeter to a centimeter apart across the cheeks.

The appointment is quick, and downtime is minimal. Expect small raised bumps at the injection points that settle within a day or two, and the usual chance of minor bruising.

## How long does Skinvive last?

Up to six months with optimal treatment. Repeat sessions maintain the effect.

That is shorter than most volumizing fillers, which is consistent with how superficially it sits and how it is metabolized.

## Can you get Skinvive and filler in the same visit?

Yes, and the combination makes sense more often than either alone. They are complementary because they work at different depths on different problems.

## What are the side effects of Skinvive?

The common reactions are local and short-lived.

- Small bumps at the injection points for a day or two
 
- Redness, tenderness, or minor bruising
 
- Temporary unevenness while the droplets settle

### Can it be reversed?

Yes. Like other hyaluronic acid products, it can be dissolved with hyaluronidase if ever needed, which is part of why HA injectables are considered a lower-risk category.

## Who is a good candidate for Skinvive?

Someone whose skin looks dull or crepey but whose facial volume is fine. Often that is a person in their late twenties to forties who is not ready for filler and does not need it.

It also suits filler patients who have volume handled and want the skin itself to match. It is not a substitute for treating deep folds or significant volume loss.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Skinvive the same as a skin booster?

Skin booster is the generic category name used internationally. Skinvive is the specific product that holds the FDA approval in the US, which matters because approval means the claims were tested against a placebo.

### How soon will you see results?

Expect the visible improvement to build over a few weeks as hydration improves, rather than an instant change on the day. Judge it at the one-month mark.

### Does Skinvive help with acne scars or pores?

It can soften the look of fine texture, but it does not treat scarring. Depressed acne scars respond better to microneedling, which works by rebuilding collagen rather than adding hydration.

### Skinvive or Botox for smoother skin?

They do different jobs. Botox relaxes the muscles that crease the skin, while Skinvive hydrates the skin itself. Lines caused by expression need Botox; texture and dullness point to Skinvive.

## Which one do you actually need?

If the shape of your face has changed, that is a filler conversation. If the shape is fine but the skin surface looks tired, that is Skinvive.
