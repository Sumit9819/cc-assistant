Something to be aware of: stock and prices

We checked all 402 products on the website. 60 are marked out of stock and 24 show no price. That is about one product in five.

Most of those do not matter, because nobody is searching for them. These ones do. They are appearing in Google regularly, and people who click land on something they cannot buy:

Hornwort is the clearest example. It sits at the very top of page one in Google, higher than anything else on the site, and it has been out of stock. All the soil, gravel and mulch products show “Available in-store!” instead of a price, so someone comparing local yards has nothing to compare.

We understand some of this is seasonal. Pond plants, fish and sod come back in spring. Knowing which are seasonal and which are gone for good lets us handle each page the right way.

What would help us most

1. Can soil, gravel and mulch show a price, even a “from” price per yard?
2. Is bike storage coming back? The bike storage page is the most-seen page on the whole site and currently lists one patio box.
3. Roughly when do pond plants and fish return, so we can say so on the page instead of leaving it blank?
