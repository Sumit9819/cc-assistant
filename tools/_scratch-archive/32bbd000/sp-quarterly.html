Quarterly Report  SP-2026-Q3
Sid's Ponds  ·  June, July and August 2026 against March, April and May 2026  ·  sids-ponds.com
  • Clicks from Google fell 21%. 4,177 visits across June, July and August against 5,288 across March, April and May, a fall of 1,111. The previous quarter contained the spring peak: May alone brought 2,598 clicks, the strongest month of the year, and that single month is most of the difference between the two quarters.
  • One page carries 41% of the fall. Secure Outdoor Storage dropped from 749 clicks to 276. Its average position over the same period improved from 11.4 to 9.6, so this is not a page that lost its rankings. Set it aside and the rest of the site moved from 4,664 clicks to 3,987.
  • Searches by name held steady while unbranded search fell. Branded searches brought 1,419 clicks against 1,480 last quarter, a fall of 4%. Searches that never mention the business moved from 1,385 to 910. The site was found for 8,600 distinct searches, slightly more than the 8,504 of the previous quarter.

03

Work completed this quarter

  • Eight category and content pages rewritten across August. The outdoor lighting department, Inground & Recessed Lighting from 70 to 355 words, Fire Pit Inserts from 67 to 333, Pond Supplies & Kits from 176 to 306, and Green Living Wall Kits rebuilt around modular wall systems. Customer questions added to Hardscaping & Interlock, the homepage search listing rewritten, and the Backyard Interlock guide restructured with design-pattern and small-yard sections.
  • Two factual errors corrected on product information. The living wall pot-set quantities were understated on two of the three kits, checked against the live product options. Two photographs on the interlock guide carried descriptions of a different product.
  • Sunday opening hours corrected on the contact page and in the hours panel that appears at the top of every page.
  • A 110KB font file removed from every page of the site. The footer payment and social icons were rebuilt as inline graphics, so the file no longer downloads anywhere.
  • Sixteen internal links repointed across the lighting pages, so each now goes straight to its destination rather than through a redirect.
  • Three readability faults fixed. Testimonial job titles on About Us were below the contrast standard at 3.12:1 and are now 4.82:1; the promotion badge text was 2.95:1 and is now 7.40:1; the location map had no description for screen readers.
  • The homepage seasonal promotion rebuilt and renamed End of Summer Blowout. Its heading had been set in a typeface the site does not load, so it had never displayed as designed, and the sale price was styled more faintly than the crossed-out original price.
  • Sixteen months of daily search history assembled, so page-level changes can now be measured against a site-wide control rather than judged in isolation.

These figures do not yet reflect that work. All of the content and technical work above was published between 19 and 29 August, the final eleven days of the quarter. The performance in this report describes the site largely before those changes took effect, and the next report is the first that can measure them.

04

Priorities for the next quarter

1. Move the living wall range onto page one

The category draws 7,836 unbranded impressions a quarter across 520 distinct searches, and six of its commercial terms sit between positions 15 and 20 — the top of page two, where a result is seen and rarely clicked. green wall has already moved from 23.3 to 13.2. This is the shortest distance to page one anywhere on the site.

2. Finish the category rewrites

Four departments were rebuilt in August. The remaining pond and landscaping categories follow the same pattern: real descriptions of what is stocked, headings drawn from the searches customers actually type, and the regional vocabulary the current pages miss.

3. Recover the homepage and fertilizer positions

After Secure Outdoor Storage, the largest falls were the homepage at 291 clicks and the Magic Carpet fertilizer page at 183. Both slipped roughly three places over the quarter, the homepage from 7.6 to 10.1 and the fertilizer page from 6.7 to 9.7. That is the difference between the top of page one and the bottom of it, and on pages of this volume those places are worth more than any new page would be.
