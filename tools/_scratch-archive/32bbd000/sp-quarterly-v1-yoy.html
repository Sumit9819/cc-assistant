01

Executive summary

  • Excluding one page, the site is flat year-on-year. Total clicks fell 17.3% against the same quarter last year, but a single product category — Secure Outdoor Storage — accounts for 81% of that decline. Every other page on the site combined moved just −4.1%.
  • Brand demand is completely stable. Searches for Sid's Ponds by name returned 37,285 impressions this quarter against 37,381 a year ago, a change of −0.3%. The audience that knows the business is intact.
  • The landscaping, pond and tools ranges are all growing. One hundred and sixty pages gained clicks year-on-year, led by Hardscaping & Interlock at +45, the living wall guide and a stone-lifting tool at +21 each, Soil at +20, Sod at +19 and Green Living Wall Kits at +14. The category rewrites completed in August are too recent to appear in these figures.

Why the impression fall overstates the picture

Impressions fell far faster than clicks, and most of that loss sits in positions that never earned traffic. Search terms ranking below position 20 account for 80% of the impression loss but only 5% of the click loss. Terms below position 50 lost 33,001 impressions year-on-year and gained 15 clicks.

The click rate on non-branded search actually improved by 24%, from 0.74% to 0.92%. The site is being shown for fewer irrelevant searches and converting the remaining ones better.

Living wall is the strongest growth position on the site

The category page drew 7,836 non-branded impressions this quarter across 520 distinct search terms. Six of its commercial terms sit between positions 15 and 20, which is the top of page two: outdoor living wall at 15.0, living wall indoor at 15.4, indoor living wall at 17.1, living wall at 17.5, indoor living wall kits at 17.7 and living wall kit at 19.8. A seventh, green wall, has already reached 13.2 from 40.9 a year ago.

The category page and the how-to guide are cleanly separated: the guide holds the instructional searches, the category holds the buying searches. Both gained clicks year-on-year.

Ranking gains were also recorded on terms that previously sat well down the results, including pond supplies toronto from position 19.8 to 4.5, green wall from 40.9 to 13.2, sod from 27.8 to 15.1, slab grabber from 24.8 to 12.1 and pond liner from 10.5 to 6.9.

06

Reading the quarter

Almost all of the content and technical work above was published between 19 and 29 August, the final eleven days of a ninety-day reporting window. The performance figures in this report therefore describe the site largely before that work took effect, and none of the gains listed in section 04 can yet be attributed to it.

In those eleven days the two rebuilt pages with existing traffic both moved ahead of the site trend — Green Living Wall Kits from 8 clicks to 12, and Hardscaping & Interlock from 7 to 10, against a site-wide movement of −12.4% over the same days last year. Eleven days and single-figure click counts are not evidence, and are recorded here only as a starting position. The rewrites will be measured properly at the twenty-eight day mark.

Work continuing into next quarter centres on the living wall range, where six commercial search terms sit at the top of page two and are the closest thing on the site to a page-one opportunity, and on completing the category rewrites across the pond and landscaping departments.

Method. All figures from Google Search Console for sids-ponds.com, retrieved 29 August 2026. Windows are equal-length 90-day periods: 1 Jun – 29 Aug 2026, the prior quarter 3 Mar – 31 May 2026, and the same period last year 2 Jun – 30 Aug 2025. Partial months are never compared against full ones.

Site totals are taken unfiltered. Branded and non-branded splits are calculated across the search terms Google names in its reporting; Google withholds rare terms, which represent a consistent 41–44% of clicks in every window shown, so those splits are a share of named terms rather than absolute totals and are compared on the same basis throughout. Page-level and term-level figures are taken from page and query reporting respectively and sum slightly differently from the unfiltered site total. Average positions are impression-weighted, never an average of averages.
