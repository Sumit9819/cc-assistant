Both pages have already won the hard part. Google ranks them on page one across Canada. The problem is what shoppers find when they arrive: on one page, no bike storage; on the other, an out-of-stock plant. These are buying decisions, not website ones, so we are bringing them to you rather than working around them.

01

Bike storage: the demand is proven, the shelf is empty

The Secure Outdoor Storage page is the single most-seen page on the site. Over 90 days it appeared in 21,838 searches across 1,096 different search terms, almost all of them for bike sheds and bike storage. It ranks on the first page of Google nationally.

The page currently lists one product: the Trimetals Garden Patio Box. The two Trimetals bicycle storage units it used to sell were discontinued and their pages now return “not found”.

The clearest evidence. Comparing the first half of August this year against the same days last year, this page fell from 164 clicks to 41 — while its Google position improved from 19.4 to 9.8. It is ranking roughly twice as well and earning a quarter of the visits. That gap is the stock, not the marketing. It is also the whole of the site’s year-on-year decline: every other page combined is up.

The decision

Can Trimetals bike storage be brought back into stock, or a comparable line brought in?

If yes

The demand and the Google rankings are already there and already paid for. Product pages go live and the traffic has somewhere to land.

If no

We should rewrite the page around what you do stock. That means deliberately giving up first-page national rankings for bike storage, so it is worth being sure first.

02

Hornwort: the best ranking on the whole site, unbuyable

The hornwort page holds the strongest Google positions anywhere on sids-ponds.com. It is also marked out of stock, and Google shows that status directly in the search result — which is why almost nobody clicks.

The decision

Is hornwort coming back into stock this season, and are the other pond livestock lines returning?

If yes

Marking it in stock changes what Google displays, and a number-one ranking starts converting instead of turning people away.

If no

Let us know and we will handle the page properly, so a top ranking stops leading customers to a dead end.

03

Why this is worth your time

Search work builds visibility. These two pages already have it — a first-page national ranking for bike storage and a number-one ranking for hornwort are assets that took years to earn and cannot be bought back quickly once lost. Right now both are pointed at products that cannot be purchased, which is the one problem no amount of content or technical work can solve.

Either answer is workable. What we would like to avoid is leaving them as they are, because Google gradually demotes pages that people click and immediately leave.
