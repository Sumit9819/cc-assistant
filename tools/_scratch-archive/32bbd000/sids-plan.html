The six rules

  1. Every editorial link points at a canonical URL.Not an alias that returns 200 and then canonicals somewhere else. This is the one rule the site was actually breaking, and the two queued changes below fix it.
  2. Local service intent belongs to the Tier 1 hub, never a sub-category.outdoor lighting mississauga and landscape lighting mississauga are the lighting page’s to win. A product sub-category chasing them lands at position 60–90 and takes nothing.
  3. A blog post links down into category; a category links back to exactly one guide.One link each way keeps the loop closed without turning descriptions into link farms.
  4. Inline only, inside a sentence that earns it.No “related guides” blocks, no link rails. If the sentence would not exist without the link, cut the link.
  5. Anchor text is the target’s query, not its menu name.pond pumps and filters, not Pumps & Filters.
  6. A category with nothing to sell gets no new links.Pointing more equity at Secure Outdoor Storage makes the empty-shelf problem more visible, not less. Stock first, links after.

5. Lighting sub-categories — dropped after checking

Dropped

This was in the plan on the strength of Inground & Recessed averaging position 61.6. Splitting its queries by intent shows why that number is misleading: eight local-service queries average 73.9, ten product-type queries average 44.5, and its own best term outdoor recessed lighting sits at 32.9. The 61.6 is an average across queries the page should never have ranked for, which is exactly the artifact I have a standing rule against diagnosing from.

It fails here. pot lights is the Canadian term and carries 398 impressions at position 40–53 — but the page already uses “pot light” three times, including a dedicated FAQ that says outright “if you have been searching for exterior pot lights, this is the range.” Coverage is present and the ranking is still bad, so vocabulary is not the constraint on this page. Nothing here is worth doing.

Honesty conditions

How we will know this is right

The previous plan committed to a falsification test after 28 days. It failed earlier than that, from analysis rather than a live test, and the plan changed accordingly. The same standard applies to what is left.

ClaimWhat would prove it wrong
Living wall is genuine striking distance28 days after the rewrite the six page-two queries have not moved and clicks are flat. That would mean the constraint there is domain strength, not the page.
Missing vocabulary is why the system family ranks at 42Already half-wounded. The same theory failed on Inground & Recessed, where pot lights vocabulary is present and the family still sits at 44.5. If the living wall system family does not move either, vocabulary coverage is not a lever on this site and #390 is the last of these I write.
Inventory is the binding constraint, not SEOBike stock arrives, the page fills, and clicks stay near 179. That would mean the ranking was never as monetisable as position 9.8 implies.
Canonical link hygiene is worth doing but smallIf #388 alone lifts the lighting sub-categories out of the 60–70 band, I underrated internal linking and the falsification above needs revisiting.

Measurement runs at 28 days on clicks, not impressions and not average position. Impressions carry a known reporting artifact this year, and average position moves when a page starts ranking for more queries, which reads as a decline while things are actually improving.
