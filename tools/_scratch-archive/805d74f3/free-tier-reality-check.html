Free Tier Reality Check

What is actually free for an AI video pipeline in August 2026, checked against primary sources rather than the blogs that summarise them.

Google AI Studio does not solve the image problem

Third-party guides claim 500 free images per day from Nano Banana. Google's own pricing page lists every Gemini image model as “Not available” on the free tier. Imagen 4 was deprecated and shut down on 17 August 2026 — three days ago. What Google does give away free is text and speech, not pictures.

Trap worth naming. MusicGen appears on most “free open-source music” lists. Its output licence is CC BY-NC 4.0 — non-commercial. Running it on your own hardware does not grant commercial rights. A monetised channel using MusicGen output is in breach.

The compute question underneath all three

Every free option above that beats the paid ones — Flux.1 Schnell, ACE-Step, Chatterbox — needs a GPU. Your machine has 5.85 GB of RAM and no CUDA, so all three would run on free cloud GPU: Kaggle offers a guaranteed 30 GPU-hours per week, Colab a variable 15–30.

Open question, and I would not build on it until it is answered. Kaggle's terms do not clearly address commercial use of notebook outputs, and I could not verify it from primary sources. Generating assets for your own creative work is one thing; running a free research platform as a production render farm for a monetised channel is a different claim. Worth reading their Terms and Acceptable Use directly before this becomes load-bearing.

What I would do next

Ordered by value per unit of effort, not by how interesting they are to build.

  1. Music bed, from a manually built libraryPixabay has no music API, so this is a one-time manual download into library/music/, then automatic sidechain ducking under the narration. Free, needs no GPU, and it is the largest perceptual gain still available.
  2. Remotion overlays fed by the sourced data tableAlready planned as Phase 4. Real cited figures on screen are what stop the video reading as narrated stock — and they are the substantive modification the inauthenticity policy asks for.
  3. Check your own AI Studio rate-limit pageTwo minutes of your time settles whether Gemini TTS can carry emotion for us, or whether Kokoro stays the ceiling. I cannot see your project's limits.
  4. Leave AI images alone for nowGoogle's free image tier does not exist, Flow's 3/day is not workable, and the Flux path depends on the unresolved Kaggle question. Stock plus motion graphics covers more than it appears to.

Verified against Google's Gemini API pricing and rate-limit documentation, model licences, and the nlm CLI surface.
