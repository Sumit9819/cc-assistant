What actually changed

Every one of these was visible in your reference folder before I looked at it properly.

Duotone, then halftone

Nothing keeps its own colour. Every asset is remapped onto a two-stop ramp from the frame’s palette and screened with a dot pitch set in final frame pixels, so one screen runs across the whole composition. Naturalistic colour was the giveaway — the eye reads mismatched light as things pasted together.

Three colours. That is the whole budget.

A red field, one duotone, one accent. The earlier versions carried red, white, gold, naturalistic copper and green trees, and looked it.

A ground with something in it

Radial rays in a near shade of the field. A smooth gradient reads as a software default; a field with structure reads as a decision someone made.

Hard shadows, never soft ones

A blurred drop shadow is the single loudest tell that elements were dragged onto a canvas. Contact is made by overlap and a tight offset, the way print does it.

Everything bleeds off an edge

Nothing floats in the middle with air around it. The coin leaves the frame, the dome leaves the frame, the columns leave the frame.

A real poster face

Barlow Condensed is too light and too narrow to carry a headline. Anton is the weight the job needs — fetched to the project under the same open licence as the rest — then speckle-masked so the letterforms read as printed.

At 128 pixels

The real difference in a feed is not legibility — the old ones were legible. It is that these have colour presence. A red field carries at a size where a dark gradient turns to sludge.

The four, full size

One bug worth knowing about, because it cost a full render pass. The speckle mask on the type made every headline vanish — no error, no warning, just gone. Chromium treats every file:// URL as an opaque origin, and a CSS mask demands a CORS-clean image, so a mask loaded by relative path fails and takes its element with it. Background images are exempt, which is why the grain plate rendered fine and the headline did not. Masks are inlined as data URIs now, and it is written down.
