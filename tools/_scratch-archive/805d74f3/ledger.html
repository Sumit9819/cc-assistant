4. Rules the frames taught us

  • Hook, 2026: open on a place and a date, no title, no question; the first turn inside 20 s with a visual change; a loop planted early and paid in the last chapter; first person in every chapter.
  • Motion, 2026: type reveals information in time with the voice. It types, un-blurs, counts, swaps and holds 1.5 to 13 s. No scale bounce anywhere, Hormozi included. Flashes go in, never out.
  • Place: references cover 25 to 60% of the frame width; ours ran 88 to 94% before the fix. Mixed case by default; caps only for one giant line or a label.
  • Cut: the picture changes on the word, to the thing named. Median reference shot 1.5 to 3 s; ours 5 s mean in the first minute, slower on purpose after “too fast”.
  • Cards: a card must add information; emphasis cards are refused. Frame-taking cards start on the cut and hold to it.
  • Sound: references sit at −16 to −21 LUFS; ours −15.3 to −15.5, target −14 / −1.5 dBTP.
  • Facts: figures only from primary pages, logged in sources/verification.md; no fabricated documents; no identifiable real person generated.

5. Checks and tools

tools/check_script.py

Script gate before the voice: a scene and a turn in the first 60 words, turns at or above 0.8 per 100 words, first line under 14 words, no “so/then” openers, first person per chapter, figures as a warning.

tools/qa_master.py

Master gate: runts and read floors on the real segment, chapter lead, text zone measured from each overlay’s alpha, text hold, slug and mark collision, repeated clips, small type, odd cut gaps (--scdet). Writes qa_report.json.

tools/study_video.py, study_type.py, study_align.py, study_script.py

Measure a reference: cuts and pace, one-frame-a-second scans, 20 fps bursts, pause structure, tension signals per 100 words.

tools/text_extent.py, keyword_frames.py

How wide type runs in any frame; a frame at every keyword moment of a master, labelled.

tools/rerender_keywords.py, sweep_orphan_keywords.py, finish_rebuild.sh

Cheap re-render of overlays after a template change; cleanup after a re-plan; resume chain after a voice quota reset.

motion/capture_doc.mjs

Captures a .gov page as document footage with the quoted line located; MIN_COL widens the column for tables.
