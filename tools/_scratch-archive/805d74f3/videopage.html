The research changed the video. My draft said ending the penny required an act of Congress. The sources say otherwise: the President directed the Treasury to stop in February 2025, the Mint stopped buying blanks in May, and the last circulating penny was struck on 12 November 2025 — while the bill to formalise it passed the House in July 2026 and the Senate in August and still has not been signed. That was not a small slip. It was the whole fourth section, and it was backwards. Nothing now reaches the narration stage until the notebook has been queried on it.
