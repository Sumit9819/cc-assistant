Every one uses the same figure and the same two photographs, so what you are rating is layout, not which picture is prettier.

One thing to fix before any of these ships. The photographs here are generated, which is right for testing a layout and wrong for the video. Cash for Clunkers was a federal programme, so real photographs of it exist and US federal works are public domain — the shipped thumbnail should use the archive. A real photograph of the real thing is both more honest and, for this niche, more persuasive.
