A

Traffic recovery: the URL migration fix

● LIVE JULY 31

The spring URL restructure had left 29 old page addresses returning “not found” — addresses that had earned roughly 2,600 clicks over the prior 16 months, about 30% of all historical search traffic, including the site's single biggest legacy page (the old mini skid steer category, 885 clicks). Google kept sending people to dead ends.

We mapped every dead URL to its correct successor page and shipped 34 permanent (301) redirects, then re-verified each one live: every redirect resolves in a single hop, with no chains and no dead ends remaining. The authority those old pages earned now flows to the pages that replaced them.

B

Every product page rebuilt to full depth

✓ 21 PAGES

Each of the 21 equipment pages now follows one proven structure, built out page by page through July:

  • “What is it built for?” — a buyer-decision section answering the question searchers actually ask
  • Lineup comparison table — price, engine, capacity and key specs against sibling models, so buyers can step up or down without leaving the site
  • Warranty, financing & dealer support block with a clear call to action
  • FAQ section answering the questions Canadian contractors search for

Alongside the structure: a single correct headline (H1) on every page and a standardized price display (“$XX,XXX CAD”) across the lineup.

C

Catalog accuracy pass & manufacturer citations

✓ 22 MACHINES + 6 CATEGORIES

Every specification on every machine page and category page was checked line-by-line against the official 2026 Mammoth catalog — engines, capacities, dimensions, weights — and corrected where the site had drifted from the spec sheet. Five discontinued models were confirmed absent and their old URLs folded into the redirect work in Section A.

To strengthen trust signals, 13 pages now cite the component manufacturers directly (Cummins, Honda, Kubota, Yanmar) — the exact engines named on each machine's spec sheet, linked to authoritative sources.

D

Hidden product content made visible: X‑Loader 2000MT

✓ TEMPLATE FIX

The 2000MT product page had 654 words of specifications, FAQ and buying copy that never rendered — the product page template was missing the element that displays a product's description. We fixed it at the template level and rebuilt the presentation to match the machine pages (styled spec table, FAQ accordion, correct dealer buttons), so the page's full content is now visible to buyers and to Google.
