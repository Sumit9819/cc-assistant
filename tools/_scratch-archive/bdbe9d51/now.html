Common Questions

Straight Answers from a Canadian Equipment Brand.

How long is the Mammoth Machinery warranty?

Every Mammoth Machinery machine carries a 5-Year / 3,000-Hour limited warranty, whichever comes first. It covers hydraulic systems, chassis, and structural components, making it one of the strongest warranties in the compact heavy equipment Canada market. The engine and powertrain are warranted directly by the engine manufacturer — Kubota or Yanmar — under their own terms, not by Mammoth. Register your machine within 30 days of delivery to secure the full term; if you register later than that, coverage is dated from the machine's manufacture or shipment date instead. Full terms are listed on the warranty page.

Where is Mammoth Machinery equipment manufactured?

Mammoth Machinery compact heavy equipment is designed, engineered, and assembled in Burlington, Ontario, Canada. Canadian-based manufacturing means faster parts availability, stronger local service support, and machines purpose-built for Canadian climate and job-site conditions.

What types of compact heavy equipment does Mammoth sell?

Mammoth Machinery sells six categories of compact equipment Canadian contractors rely on: mini skid steers (X-Loader series), compact wheel loaders, mini excavators, tracked mini dumpers, wheeled mini dumpers, and full-size track loaders.

Does Mammoth Machinery offer equipment financing?

Yes. Mammoth offers equipment financing with flexible terms on approved credit, available to both established fleets and new construction or landscaping businesses across Canada. See full options on the financing page.

What is a mini dumper?

A mini dumper is a compact powered wheelbarrow used to transport heavy loads such as soil, gravel, concrete, or debris across job sites that are too tight for a full-size dumper. Mammoth builds both tracked mini dumpers for soft or uneven terrain and wheeled mini dumpers for hard, flat surfaces.

What size mini excavator do I need?

For residential landscaping and tight access work, a 2-ton mini excavator is usually sufficient. For utility trenching, foundations, and small commercial projects, choose a 3 to 3.5-ton model for added digging depth and lift capacity. Mammoth's mini excavator lineup covers both.
