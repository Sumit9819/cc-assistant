Mammoth Machinery  ·  August 26, 2026  ·  mammothmachinery.ca
Five things we need from you to finish work already underway. Each one is blocking something specific on the website. If you can answer these in one go, we can close all of them this week.

1. Price for the X-Loader 2000MT

What we need: the CAD price, or a "from" price if it varies by configuration.

Every other machine on the site shows its price publicly, and that is a genuine advantage: most competitors hide prices behind a quote form. The 2000MT is one of two machines with no price anywhere.

What it unblocks: we can add a starting price to the Full Size Track Loaders listing in Google, the way we did for the dumper categories. Those listings are the ones buyers see before they click.

2. Price for the MTL1000 Track Loader

What we need: the CAD price, or confirmation that it is quote-only and why.

The MTL1000 page currently says "Request a Quote for Pricing". It is the only machine on the site that does. Its full specification table is published, so a buyer can see the 74 HP Kubota engine and the 2,600 lb capacity but not what it costs.

3. Is the MTL1000 currently available to buy?

What we need: a yes or no, and if it is special-order or not yet in stock, we should say so on the page.

It is presented in the Full Size Track Loaders lineup as a standard option, with a full spec table. But it has no price, no photographs of the actual machine, and no spec sheet, while every other model has all three. If it is not currently sellable, buyers are being invited to enquire about something they cannot get.

4. Photographs of two machines

What we need: product photos of the MTL1000 Track Loader and the MT2850CB Tracked Mini Dumper. Three or four angles each is plenty; phone photos in good light are fine.

Neither machine has a single photograph anywhere in the website's library. Their pages currently show a different machine instead: the MTL1000 page shows an X-Loader 3000MT, and the MT2850CB page shows a wheeled dumper. We corrected seven other pages that had the same problem this month by swapping in each machine's own photos, but for these two there was nothing to swap in.

5. How does warranty registration actually happen?

What we need: a short answer on the current process. Do dealers register machines on the customer's behalf, does someone do it manually, or is the website form meant to be the route?

The warranty registration form on the website has received no submissions in the last six months. Registration within 30 days is what activates the 5-year / 3,000-hour coverage, so if machines are going out unregistered that is a risk to both the customer and to you.

We also found and fixed a related fault this week: registration notifications were not reaching Mammoth at all. They now arrive at info@mammothmachinery.ca. Customers were always receiving their own confirmation email.

Why these five and not a longer list: everything else currently in progress is work we can do ourselves. These are the only items that need information only you have.
