August is running 24% ahead of July's record pace just nine days in. The work below happened this sprint: the content engine restarted, every high-traffic search snippet rebuilt on real query data, and the first wave of site speed work shipped.

01

Content Engine: Two New Buyer Guides Live, Weekly Schedule Locked

Published Aug 11

How Does Equipment Financing Work in Canada?

Full buyer's guide: loan vs lease, what lenders check, application steps. Every fact verified against BDC (Canada's business development bank), with expert quotes from a named BDC account manager, linked to source.

Published Aug 12

What Does a 5-Year Equipment Warranty Actually Cover?

Coverage explained in plain language: the year/hour dual clock, what the machine maker vs the engine maker covers, registration rules. Anchored entirely to Mammoth's published warranty terms.

Both guides match the site's design system, cross-link each other and the money pages, and target the financing and warranty questions buyers research before purchase. The publishing calendar is now locked: one guide every Monday through September 21. The third guide, “Mini Excavator vs Mini Skid Steer”, is written, built, and set for Monday Aug 17.
