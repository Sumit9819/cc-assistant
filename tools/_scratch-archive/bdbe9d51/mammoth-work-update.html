WORK UPDATE  MM-2026-08-P3
Mammoth Machinery  ·  Work completed Aug 19–23, 2026  ·  mammothmachinery.ca
17 PAGES UPDATED  ·  1 NEW GUIDE PUBLISHED  ·  ALL LIVE AND CHECKED ON THE WEBSITE
This update covers three kinds of work. Six category pages had text on them that customers literally could not see. Eight machine pages had photo problems, including seven pages showing the wrong machine. Four guides had their Google listings rewritten, and one new guide was published. There is a short note at the end about two missing prices and one missing set of photos, which is currently blocking work.
01

Category pages: text nobody could read

mammothmachinery.ca/full-size-track-loaders/
Full Size Track Loaders — three labels were completely invisible

Dark text had been placed on a dark background, so three labels were invisible on screen, including the very first label at the top of the page. Four more labels were white text on white or grey. Two headline numbers were in a green too pale to read.

Nobody had reported it because there was nothing visible to report. The words were in the page, just not readable.

BEFORE9 pieces of text unreadable or invisible. Page check: 69 out of 100.
AFTERAll text readable. Page check: 88 out of 100.
mammothmachinery.ca/wheel-loaders/
Wheel Loaders — the warranty card could not be read

Five section labels were invisible white-on-white. Three lines on the green warranty card, including "5 Years / 3,000 Hours", were too pale against the green to read properly. This is the page's main trust message.

BEFORE13 pieces of text unreadable. Page check: 69 out of 100.
AFTERAll text readable. Page check: 83 out of 100.

Mini Excavators — four invisible labels, plus a link added

Four section labels were invisible, and the two headline numbers ("3 Models" and "10 ft 11 in") were unreadable green on white. We also added a link to the excavator versus skid steer guide, for buyers who have not decided which type of machine they need.

02

Machine pages: seven were showing the wrong machine

The main product photo on seven machine pages was a photo of a different machine: a wheeled dumper appearing on pages selling tracked machines, and on the concrete buggy page. One page had no main photo at all. A customer comparing models was looking at the same wrong picture on almost every page.

Each page now shows its own machine, using a photo already in the website's library. We also added written descriptions to 41 photos across these pages. Those descriptions are what Google Images and screen-reading software use to tell what a picture shows; they are not visible on the page.

03

Guides: two competing pages separated, one new guide

mammothmachinery.ca/mini-skid-steer-vs-full-size/
Mini vs Full-Size Skid Steer — stopped it competing with the product page

Google had started showing this guide instead of the Mini Skid Steers product page when people searched to buy. Over one week the product page dropped from 193 appearances in Google for that search to 34, while this guide went from 34 to 428.

That is the wrong way round. Someone ready to buy was landing on a comparison article instead of the page with machines and prices. We reworded the title and the main heading so the two pages no longer compete, and added a link from the first paragraph straight to the product page.

BEFOREMini Skid Steer vs Full Size: Best 2026 Canada Guide
AFTERMini vs Full-Size Skid Steer: Which to Buy in Canada
mammothmachinery.ca/what-is-a-concrete-buggy/
What Is a Concrete Buggy — added the other two names buyers use

People search for this machine under three names: concrete buggy, power buggy and georgia buggy. The listing used only the first one, so the other searches saw nothing familiar. All three are now in the listing, along with the concrete weight figure the page explains.

BEFOREConcrete Buggy Canada: Best 2026 Uses & Specs Guide
AFTERConcrete Buggy Canada: Power Buggy Uses, Capacity, Specs
mammothmachinery.ca/landscaping-jobs-mini-skid-steer/

The plain-language warranty guide had no links pointing to it from anywhere on the website, so almost nobody was finding it. The warranty page now links to it in the opening paragraph. That same sentence had a grammar mistake, which is corrected.

Something to be aware of: two prices and one machine's photos

Adding real prices to the Google listings is working well on the other categories, and we wanted to do the same for the track loaders. We could not, because two machines have no price published anywhere on the website:

MachineWhat is missingWhat it blocks
X-Loader 2000MTNo price on its page or product listingCannot show a "from" price for Full Size Track Loaders in Google
MTL1000 Track LoaderNo price on its pageSame, and the page cannot answer the first question buyers ask
MT2850CB Tracked Mini DumperNo photographs of this machine exist in the website libraryIts main photo still shows a different machine, and we have nothing correct to replace it with

Every other machine on the site has its price published, and that is a genuine advantage over competitors who hide prices behind a quote form. These three are the gaps in it.

05

What would help us most

  1. The price for the X-Loader 2000MT and the MTL1000, even an approximate "from" price, so the track loader pages can show what every other category shows.
  2. Photographs of the MT2850CB, so its page can stop showing a different machine.
  3. Confirmation that the dealer list is complete. The Find a Dealer page currently lists head office plus seven dealer partners.
Every change above was applied to the live website between Aug 19 and Aug 23, 2026, and confirmed afterwards by loading each page and checking the result. Search figures come from Google Search Console, comparing the weeks of Aug 7–13 and Aug 14–20, 2026.
