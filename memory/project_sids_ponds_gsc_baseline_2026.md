Client supplied 11 GSC PDF exports on 2026-07-31 (16 months, site-wide + homepage + 9 pages). The plugin's own GSC is still **not connected** (`configured:false, connected:false, rows_cached:0`), so these PDFs are the only data. Export ends **2026-07-29**, so this is the BASELINE, not a result of our work.

**Headline:** July 2025 (29d) 1,542 clicks vs July 2026 (29d) 1,202 = **-22% YoY**. Impressions -43%. But average position improved **~26 -> ~14** and CTR rose 1.93% -> 2.62%. Rank got much better while clicks fell, so **CTR is the binding constraint**, not ranking. A Sept 11-13 2025 event pruned ~35% of impressions and improved position ~13 places, which reads as dead-weight pruning, not a penalty.

**Biggest commercial finding — bike storage with no bike inventory.** The Secure Outdoor Storage category (term 393) is the #1 non-brand traffic engine: pos 6.5-7 on "bike storage" (18,640 imp), 269 clicks on "bike shed", "bike shed canada" 6.46% CTR, "asgard bike shed canada" 12.93%. The catalogue holds **exactly 1 product** (Trimetals Garden Patio Box) and a site search for "bike" returns **zero products** — the Trimetals bicycle-storage SKUs were discontinued and now 404. Demand is proven, the supplier relationship exists. Deliberately did NOT change that page's title: either direction is a client inventory decision, not an SEO edit.

**Merchant listings are the untapped lever.** Site-wide, Merchant listings = 401 clicks/4,445 imp (**9.02% CTR at pos 5.79**) vs Product snippets 8,241 clicks/574,934 imp (**1.43% at pos 21.70**). On the magic-carpet page, Merchant listings hit **32.18% CTR at pos 1.70**. No feed plugin is installed and **no product exposes sku/gtin/mpn/brand**.

**Other confirmed:** hornwort ranks pos 3.64 with 10,678 impressions and 6 clicks (0.06%) while OutOfStock — best organic position on the site attached to unbuyable stock (koi, goldfish, tropical marginals also out). "Canada" as a qualifier multiplies CTR 3-5x across pages. Cannibalization: "pond supplies near me" spread over 7 pages. Hardscaping draws installer intent ("interloc stone company mississauga", 1,182 imp, pos 9.76, ZERO clicks) at a supplier.
