erofwhiterock.com was created as a copy of erofirving.com. The intent was to replicate the site structure + service-page inventory and rewrite content for the White Rock catchment, but the rewrite is incomplete. The user confirmed 2026-05-20 that "we have not fully changed all the content, so plagiarism might arise."

**Why this matters:**
- Service pages (especially the EN service inventory — 852 Imaging, 868 Sore Throat, 909 Bronchitis, 971 Lab Testing, 993 Food Poisoning, 1010 Back Pain, 1041 Abdominal Pain, 1072 Dehydration, 1091 Appendicitis, 1267 Rashes, 1295 Blood Clots, 1317 High Fever, 1338 Eye Injuries, 1374 Fractures, 1396 Respiratory, 1425 Head Injuries, 1446 Concussion, 1467 TBI, 2453 Pediatric, 2482 Stroke, 2516 Chest Pain, 3457 Lakewood, 3505 Emergency Services, 3635 Lake Highlands, 3650 Garland) are most likely to contain unmodified ER of Irving content with the city name swapped.
- The recent blog posts published 2026-05-11 to 2026-05-18 (4986 Pillar 1, 5032 Cardio pillar, 5054 Bone/Joint pillar, 5061 Stomach pillar, 5091-5094 Heat/Drown/WorldCup/Tick, 5134 Pillar 6, etc.) were freshly written for erofwhiterock and have lower duplication risk.
- Google's scaled-content-abuse policy (2024-03-05) flags "automated transformations like synonymizing or translating" — city-name swaps without substantive rewrite are exactly this pattern.

**How to apply:**
1. **Both sites are MCP-accessible.** `mcp__cc-assistant-erofirving-com__*` tools work directly on the source. Use them to diff pages: pull the Irving counterpart of any erofwhiterock service page and verify duplication before rewriting.
2. **Rewrite priority:** service pages > Spanish translations of service pages > blog posts. The service pages are the money pages AND the highest duplication risk.
3. **Add unique information gain** to every rewrite: real CDC/NIH/Texas DSHS stats framed for the White Rock / Lake Highlands / East Dallas catchment. Local stats > Irving stats. Use specific North Dallas data, not Irving data.
4. **Structure improvement is in scope** — user gave approval 2026-05-20 to add new sections where pages look "awful" (no proper spacing, no structure). Layout-altering changes are pre-approved for restructure work in this initiative.
5. **People-first framing:** triage-decisioning H2s + "When is this an ER visit?" patterns + clear ER vs urgent care vs primary care decision frameworks (matches the healthcare overlay v0.28 ER-specific guidance).
