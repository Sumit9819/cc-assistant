Marketing manager asked for social creative to increase reach. Built a cross-site keyword brief from the 4 GSC warehouses + Ubersuggest share data. Artifact: https://claude.ai/code/artifact/4723e465-98b0-4304-a7c4-78183143e851

**Seasonality (measured, erofirving monthly clicks Dec 2025 - Aug 2026):** Aug is the FLOOR of the year, Mar the peak. Total 2,096 Dec / 5,128 Mar / 1,342 Aug. Illness cluster 88 Aug -> 941 Mar = 10.7x. Injury 549 Aug -> 1,885 Mar. So ER content creative must be BUILT Sept-Nov to land ahead of the Oct-Mar curve.

**Platform split (Ubersuggest share counts on best third-party page per theme):**
- Facebook = ER symptom/seasonal explainers (poison ivy 128,430; what-to-eat-when-sick 3,943; dehydration signs 1,848).
- Pinterest = IWC metabolic/weight-loss ONLY. One insulin-resistance diet plan holds 58,958 pins vs 97 FB shares.
- Reddit = 0-1 shares across every theme tested. Do not budget for it.

**Distinctive social territory per site:**
- erofirving = national explainer reach engine (what to eat when sick 188 ck, hot potato voice 143, red circle on skin 140, hairline fracture 113).
- eroflufkin = wounds/bites/rural injury, and it is the ONLY property compounding (59 clicks Dec -> 720 Aug, +1,120%).
- erofwhiterock = "how long does X take in the ER" cluster: ~10 queries at position 1-10 with ZERO clicks. Poor blog posts, excellent social creative (the answer IS the asset).
- irvingwellnessclinic = insulin resistance + IV-for-fatigue (6,276 imp across 3 phrasings), ranking 18-32 so social is doing original reach, not amplification.

**TWO OPEN QUESTIONS - resolve before Q1 budget:**
1. Is erofirving's Mar->Aug -73.8% seasonal or structural? 9 months of data cannot separate them, and eroflufkin rose over the same window on the same content model. Needs a real diagnosis.
2. Is erofwhiterock's zero-click page-one cluster AI absorption or just thin volume? (heat stroke symptoms sits at position 2.4 with 0 clicks; er wait times near me at position 1.0 with 0 clicks.)

Exclusions written into the brief: "near me" terms (local pack, fixed by GBP + reviews, not creative), own brand queries, clinician-audience leakage (skeletal traction 67 ck, traccion esqueletica 21, distended bladder 32 = nursing students), and the AHA hypertensive-crisis strings on Lufkin (thousands of imp, 0 clicks, they want the AHA).

IWC creative must not imply physician involvement - see [[project_iwc_provider_is_aprn]]. Ranked on clicks not impressions per [[reference_gsc_impression_bug_2026]]. Relates to [[project_active_engagement]], [[reference_search_evidence_2026_08]].
