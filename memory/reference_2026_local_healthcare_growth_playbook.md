Deep-research (verified, 17 confirmed / 8 killed) on highest-impact 2026 growth levers for the portfolio. Applies to all four sites.

**Intent split (HIGH):** clinical/educational queries = answer-engine game (AI Overviews on ~49% of healthcare page-1 queries, ~92% informational); local "near me" = GBP/local-pack game (AIO only ~15%, map pack ~93%). → Build AEO clinical content (symptom guides, "is X an emergency", treatment explainers) for AI surfaces; treat "near me" as a GBP discipline. (Conductor, Whitespark)

**AI discovery is now real (HIGH):** 45% of consumers use ChatGPT/AI for local recs (up from 6%), 82% read AI review summaries, Google share 83%→71%. AI-search visibility entered the 2026 local ranking factors; 3 of top 5 AI-visibility factors are CITATION-related (earned "best of" lists, authoritative directory presence). → invest in earned citations + directories. (BrightLocal LCRS 2026, Whitespark)

**Reviews = #1 under-used lever (HIGH):** #2 local-pack ranking group (~20% wt, up from 16%), RECENCY now the dominant dimension. Consumer thresholds (BrightLocal 2026, stated-intent): 47% won't use <20 reviews; 31% only 4.5★+; 74% want reviews <3mo old; 80% favor businesses that reply to all; 81% expect reply <1wk. → continuous review-gen + same-week responses on every site. (See [[feedback_advisor_mode_ui]] for surfacing.)

**GBP: categories > posts (HIGH):** primary category = top individual factor; ~4 additional categories = best map rank. Geo-tagged photos, Q&A quantity, post frequency, description keywords = LITTLE ranking impact (Sterling Sky tests) — they still aid conversion. → maximize categories; don't burn staff time on posting/Q&A for rankings.

**Freestanding-ER billing (HIGH, CMS/ACEP primary):** No Surprises Act = insured emergency patients pay only in-network cost-sharing, EXPLICITLY covers independent freestanding EDs (carve-out: post-stabilization w/ consent). IFECs CANNOT bill Medicare/Medicaid (CMS doesn't reimburse). TX is one of ~4 states licensing IFEDs. → prominent "what you'll pay with insurance" content + steer Medicare/Medicaid appropriately. Pair with wait-time/24-7/parking convenience messaging (MEDIUM).

**Conversion economics (MEDIUM):** wellness/med-spa pages convert ~8.2% median vs 5.1% healthcare (Unbounce; use relative gap not absolutes) → Irving Wellness conversion investments (booking/speed-to-lead/financing/before-after) have highest payoff per visitor.
