When restoring or choosing images for service-page rebuilds on erofirving.com (and sister sites), NEVER use blog-post featured images. They have the article's title text baked into the graphic (e.g. "How to Tell If a Bone Is Fractured or Bruised: Signs & Tips", "Sudden Chest Pressure: When It's Not Just Heartburn") and look off-topic and unprofessional inside a page section.

**Why:** User flagged 2026-07-08 after approving Chest Pain (2516) and Fractures (1374) rebuilds: "you are using image from blog posts, which have text in it, and it does not resemble according to the topic."

**How to apply:**
- Blog featured images are recognizable by question/title-style filenames: `How-...`, `When-...`, `What-...`, `Why-[topic]-...` (note: `Why-Choose-ER-of-Irving-...` from 2025/02-03 is a PAGE image and fine), `Understanding-...`, `...-Signs-Tips`, `...-The-103°-Rule`, and by upload months that match blog publishing (2025/04+, 2025/10, 2026/03).
- Safe pools: the original service-page section images from 2025/02–2025/03 (e.g. `Emergency-Fever-Evaluation-Diagnosis`, `Advanced-Diagnostics`) and the 2026/06 `*-Service-Card-Image.webp` set (used as heroes, where the overlay hides any framing issues).
- When unsure, prefer a generic clinical photo from the page-era pool over a topically-perfect blog graphic.
