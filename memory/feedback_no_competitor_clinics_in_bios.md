When writing or rewriting a provider bio (or any staff section on a service page), only include **hospitals and academic medical centers** in the Professional Path / affiliations section. Drop any other wellness clinic, med spa, or competitive private practice — even if the provider lists it on LinkedIn.

**Why:** The saved 2026 SEO playbook rule is "NEVER cite competitor sites." Listing a competing wellness clinic on a provider bio:
1. Sends an entity-association signal to Google linking our provider to a competitor's brand
2. Helps the competitor's brand search ("trugevity", "[other clinic] dallas") surface our content as a path to learn about them
3. Dilutes our authority claim on the provider — divided practice signals divided focus

**How to apply:**
- INCLUDE: hospitals (Baylor, UT Southwestern, Medical City, etc.), academic medical centers (UChicago Medical Center, Mayo Clinic, etc.), trauma centers, university nursing/medical schools. These are pure authority signals.
- EXCLUDE: other wellness clinics, med spas, longevity practices, hormone clinics, IV bars, ketamine clinics, etc. Even if the provider is still active there.
- If the user explicitly asks to include a sister practice they own, ask once for confirmation — but default is exclude.

Reasoned wrongly that the GSC query "trugevity" (pos 20, 12 impressions) was a reason to anchor the association — backwards: those impressions are people searching for TruGevity, and us ranking for it sends them to learn about a competitor. User caught it before queue.
