**Hard rule:** NEVER place hospital-ED-comparison copy on any erofwhiterock / erofirving / eroflufkin page, including:
- "Hospital EDs average 142 min" (or any door-to-provider gap citation against hospitals)
- "Faster than hospital ER" / "Skip the hospital wait"
- Any phrasing that positions us as superior to a hospital ED by negative framing

**Why:**
- Erodes patient trust in hospitals — hospitals are the appropriate venue for trauma, ICU, and complex inpatient care, and are our REFERRAL DESTINATIONS when patients need that level of care
- Reads as fear-based marketing (skill §14 says no urgency-pressure tactics)
- Damages potential referral relationships with hospital-affiliated physicians and partners
- Can panic readers in a fever crisis
- Marketing claim sounds adversarial even when statistically true

**What IS allowed:**
- **Decision-aid comparisons** (ER vs urgent care vs pediatrician) — these help patients pick the right venue for their symptom, not knock any provider type. See S7 "Where to Go" pattern on post 1317.
- **Self-anchored facts** ("Door-to-provider under 10 minutes. Open 24/7.") — true statement about our operation, no comparison to anyone else
- **Operational reality framing** ("We don't have a trauma queue") — explains our model without referencing hospital wait times

**Design skill conflict — RESOLVED 2026-05-28:** All 3 ER skills (`erofwhiterock-design`, `erofirving-design`, `eroflufkin-design`) updated. §14 now reads self-anchored only, §18 has explicit anti-pattern banning hospital ED comparisons (even with cited stats). TL;DR header rule #8 also calls it out. Lufkin's §18 #2 strengthened — applies even to LOCAL hospitals (small-market = local hospitals are named entities + referral partners + everyone knows them).

**How to apply:**
- Any wait-time / speed / response-time copy uses self-anchored facts only
- If a stat about hospital EDs is needed (e.g., for internal SEO research), don't put it on the page
- Comparison phrasing only allowed for ER vs urgent care vs primary-care decision aids
- Same rule applies to all 3 ER sites (erofwhiterock, erofirving, eroflufkin)
