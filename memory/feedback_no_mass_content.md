"We cannot mass produce content because google will penalize us." (operator, 2026-09-25,
while approving a White Rock missing-topics scan.)

**Why:** Google's scaled-content-abuse policy (2024-03) targets many low-value pages "no matter
how created", and the ER sites are YMYL. Volume without a unique contribution risks a
sitewide quality demotion, not just a weak post. The operator is also watching for it, so a
long list of proposed posts reads as the wrong instinct.

**How to apply:**
- A gap scan's output is a shortlist of at most 3 topics, each with evidence (real search
  demand from GSC/warehouse, no existing page covering it, a clear thing the post adds that
  competitors lack). Say plainly which ones NOT to write.
- Before proposing a new post, check whether an existing page could absorb the topic
  (a section, an FAQ, a better title). Prefer the refresh.
- One post at a time through research, draft, review and publish; no batch drafting.
- Keep the spacing rules in [[feedback-calendar-is-publish-schedule]] and the city-page rule
  in [[feedback_location_page_doctrine]].
