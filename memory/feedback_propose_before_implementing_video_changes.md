After a five-hour run of fixes, re-plans and recomposes on 2026-09-05 the owner said:
"you should have told me to check what are the changes, I should have reviewed it, if
it was okay, you could have implemented, but not you directly implemented it, and it
took this long."

**Why:** the owner wants to judge each change before it costs a rebuild; a chain of
autonomous fixes on three videos produced good masters but took the afternoon and
removed the owner from the decision.

**How to apply:** for studio work, deliver a short written proposal (what, why, cost in
minutes, what it changes on screen) and STOP for approval before editing templates,
the planner, scripts or re-running plan/compose. Diagnosis, measurement and research
pages are fine to do unprompted; implementation is not. Keep each approved change
small and report after each one. Related: [[feedback_video_text_zone_and_qa_before_delivery]],
[[feedback_look_at_every_shot_before_delivering]].
