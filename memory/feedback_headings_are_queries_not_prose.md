2026-07-21, irvingwellnessclinic: operator reviewed two freshly drafted posts and called the headings out as "fillers, not optimized keywords." They were right. I had written "The Case for Pellets: Set It and Forget It", "Side by Side: Stability, Control, and Convenience", "First, Check the Dose...", "Stalled? Bring Us the Puzzle" - flow-styled magazine headings with zero query phrases.

**Why:** Headings are the retrieval layer. Google snippets, AI chunk-matching (~200-500 word chunks), and scanning readers all key off H2/H3 text. A heading without a query phrase is invisible to all three. Ordinal headings (First/Second/Third) additionally break the Island Test - the chunk is meaningless extracted alone.

**How to apply:**
1. Draft every H2 by asking: what would someone TYPE to want exactly this section? Use those words ("Hormone Pellet Therapy: Pros and Cons", "When Does Weight Loss Plateau on Semaglutide?", "How to Choose Between X, Y, and Z").
2. Banned heading shapes: "The Case for X", "Side by Side", "What You Need to Know", "First/Second/Third, ...", clever CTA headings ("Bring Us the Puzzle").
3. Voice and personality live in the BODY prose, never in the heading layer.
4. Self-check before queueing any post: read only the H2s in sequence - each must work as a standalone search result title.
