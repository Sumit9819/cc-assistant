**Priority tiers for geo-anchors in body content:**

1. **Primary (use most)** — `near White Rock Lake`, `White Rock`, neighborhood names (Lakewood, Lake Highlands, Casa View, Casa Linda, Forest Hills, Old Lake Highlands, Garland border). 5/5 SEO + brand alignment. The brand literally contains "White Rock" — every use reinforces entity SEO. Hyper-local SERP has near-zero competition.

2. **Secondary (use when broader reach helps)** — `East Dallas`, `White Rock and East Dallas`, `10-minute catchment from White Rock`. Sharper than generic "Dallas" but still pulls volume from Dallas-area searchers. Accurate (the clinic IS in East Dallas).

3. **Tertiary (sparingly, transactional blog content only)** — `Dallas-Fort Worth area`, `Dallas metro`. One mention per blog post max, only when the topic has high Dallas-volume search demand worth chasing. Never repeated as a bolded keyword phrase.

4. **NAP / schema only** — `Dallas, TX 75238` literal address + Google Business Profile alignment. Required for local SEO. Keep verbatim everywhere it represents the actual address.

5. **Avoid** — Generic `Dallas` or `Dallas, Texas` repeated 3+ times in body as the primary geo-anchor. Competes against hospital systems and other freestanding ERs across the entire metro. Dilutes the hyper-local positioning the brand owns.

**CRITICAL: location must flow grammatically, not be stuffed onto action verbs.**

Location flows naturally with **nouns that take a place**: services, facilities, brand, address.
- ✓ Good: `ER of White Rock`, `our ER near White Rock Lake`, `emergency stroke care near White Rock Lake` (service + location pair)
- ✓ Good: `the freestanding ER near White Rock Lake`, `at our facility on Northwest Highway`
- ✗ Bad: `act in time near White Rock Lake` — action verb does not take location
- ✗ Bad: `save your life near White Rock Lake`, `recover faster near White Rock Lake`, `what to do near White Rock Lake`
- ✗ Bad: appending the city/neighborhood to an action verb just to hit the keyword

**Why:** the brand name "ER of White Rock" already carries the geo signal. When the sentence has the brand or a facility noun, location is already implied or can attach there cleanly. Forcing location onto an action verb reads as keyword stuffing and the 2026 helpful-content + AI-Overview algorithms specifically penalize that pattern.

**Fix-up rule for meta descriptions and excerpts:**
- The geo signal should live in the **CTA half** of the description, attached to **the brand or the facility**, not the **problem/symptom half**.
- ✓ Good: "Spot DVT and pulmonary embolism warning signs early at ER of White Rock."
- ✗ Bad: "Spot DVT and pulmonary embolism warning signs and act in time near White Rock Lake."
