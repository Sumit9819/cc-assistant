Reports must contain **only what was done**. Leave out:

- "Waiting on you" / next-steps / anything that reads as the operator's task list
- "Blocked on the client" items — missing stock, unanswered questions, contradictory information they supplied
- Requests for data ("send me GSC for these URLs")
- My own process candour (probes that returned false results, corrections I made mid-task)

**Why:** these documents circulate. A report that reaches the client's marketing team and lists what that team has failed to do is embarrassing for them, and a report carrying the operator's private to-do list does not belong in their hands either. The operator raises blockers and asks personally, in conversation.

**How to apply:** write reports in a neutral record voice — what was found, what was built, what was measured, what shipped. Keep the analysis and the numbers, since that is the delivered value; drop the imperatives and the second person. Findings that imply a client decision get stated as observations about the site, not as things someone must go and fix. Put blockers, asks and next steps in the chat reply instead, where they belong.

Applied on sids-ponds 2026-08-12: a report was rewritten from 5 pages to 3 by removing "Waiting on you", "Blocked on the client" (bike storage stock, opening-hours conflict) and the GSC data request. See [[feedback_handoff_docs_before_after_only]] for the related rule on handoff docs.
